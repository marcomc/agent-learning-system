# Weekly Rule Discovery

This is an integrated phase, not an independent scheduled runner. The first
consolidation of the week invokes `pipeline-audit --run-id ID --weekly` before
promotion; a later consolidation retries after a missed or failed cycle.

Follow `skills/consolidate-agent-learnings/references/pipeline.md`. Preserve
open findings across incremental scans and investigate complete implicated
instructions. Use recent state-only recurrence summaries; leave unavailable
cost or effectiveness measurements unknown. Intentional repetition requires
retained coverage and saved evidence.

Consolidation owns authorized instruction changes and scoped post-verification.
The morning digest owns ordinary email. Retire the standalone weekly schedule
to avoid duplicate discovery, competing ownership and stale notifications.
